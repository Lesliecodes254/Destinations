Beach

Diani Beach

📍 Kwale County, Kenya

Diani is a world-class tropical beach stretching 17 km along the Indian Ocean coast. Its powdery white sand and warm turquoise waters make it one of Africa's most celebrated beach destinations, lined with swaying palms and vibrant coral reefs.

Beach

Watamu Beach

📍 Kilifi County, Kenya

Nestled within a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve, Watamu offers pristine white-sand beaches and one of the most biodiverse marine environments in East Africa. The Watamu Marine National Park teems with green turtles, dolphins, and 600+ fish species.

Activities
🐢 Turtle Watching 🚣 Kayaking 🐬 Dolphin Trips 🎣 Deep-Sea Fishing

National Parks

Wild landscapes, majestic wildlife, and raw natural wonder

National Park

Maasai Mara

📍 Narok County, Kenya

Home to the Great Migration — one of the world's greatest wildlife spectacles — the Maasai Mara teems with lions, elephants, and wildebeest. Lush rolling savannah and the Mara River define this iconic landscape that inspired countless documentaries.

Activities
🦁 Game Drives 🎈 Hot Air Balloon 📸 Photography 🏕️ Bush Camping 🌅 Sunset Safaris
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5.0 / 5
National Park

Amboseli National Park

📍 Kajiado County, Kenya

Set against the magnificent backdrop of Mount Kilimanjaro, Amboseli is famous for its large free-ranging elephant herds. The park's open plains, swamps, and woodlands create a mosaic of habitats that support extraordinary wildlife diversity.

Activities
🐘 Elephant Viewing 🦒 Game Drives 🏔️ Kilimanjaro Views 🐦 Bird Watching

Nairobi

📍 Nairobi County, Kenya

Africa's "Green City in the Sun" is a thrilling metropolis where skyscrapers meet wildlife. The only city in the world with a national park on its doorstep, Nairobi offers world-class dining, tech innovation, vibrant nightlife, and an unmatched safari gateway experience.

Cultural Sites

Ancient history, rich traditions, and timeless heritage

Cultural Site

Lamu Old Town

📍 Lamu County, Kenya

A UNESCO World Heritage Site and East Africa's oldest, best-preserved Swahili settlement. Lamu's labyrinthine coral-stone streets, ornate wooden doors, and dhow harbour tell 700 years of Swahili civilisation. No cars — just donkeys and the sea breeze.

Activities
⛵ Dhow Sailing 🚶 Heritage Walks 🎨 Art Galleries 🍛 Swahili Cuisine 🕌 Fort Visit
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.9 / 5
Cultural Site

Fort Jesus, Mombasa

📍 Mombasa County, Kenya

Built by the Portuguese in 1593 and now a UNESCO World Heritage Site, Fort Jesus dominates the old harbour of Mombasa. This coral-stone fortress witnessed centuries of epic battles between Portuguese, Omanis, and the British, and its museum holds extraordinary Swahili artefacts.

Activities
🏰 Guided Tours 🏛️ Museum 📖 History Walks 🌊 Harbour Views
⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4.7 / 5
